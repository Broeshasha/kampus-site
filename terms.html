These are the rules for using KampusLink. By creating an account or using the app, you agree to them. If you are under 18, please read them with a parent or guardian.

1. What KampusLink is

KampusLink is a free study app for students in Algeria. It has short lessons, flashcards, quizzes, exercises, mock exams, module chats and an XP shop. It works offline once your content is downloaded. KampusLink is run by OPERATOR: Mpofu Professor, Sidi amar 19 Mai, Algeria.

KampusLink is independent. It is not affiliated with or approved by the Ministry of Higher Education, the Ministry of National Education, the ONEC, any university or any school. It does not replace your teachers, your courses or official information.

2. Who can use it

  • KampusLink is for university students and secondary-school (lycée) students.
  • If you are under 18, you need the permission of your parent or legal guardian to use KampusLink.
  • You must be at least 13 years old.
  • One account per person. The information you give during setup must be true.

3. Your account

  • You sign in with Google. Keep your Google account safe; you are responsible for what happens in your account.
  • Choose a username that does not impersonate anyone and does not contain insults, personal information or links.
  • If you share a phone, sign out when you finish.
  • You can delete your account at any time in the app (see Delete Account).

4. Our study content

  • Lessons, cards and questions are written by the KampusLink team, partly with the help of AI tools, and checked against the official programmes. They can still contain mistakes. Use the report button when you find one; we fix reported mistakes.
  • Past baccalauréat questions are shown with their source (year and stream). They come from official exam papers. Our worked answers are our own and are not official corrections.
  • KampusLink helps you study. It does not guarantee any grade or exam result.
  • The content is for your personal study. Do not copy, sell or republish it in bulk, and do not use scripts or bots to download it.

5. What you post

You keep ownership of the messages and reports you post. You allow us to display them to the students of the same module, to translate them automatically, and to moderate them. Follow the Community Guidelines. We may remove any message that breaks them.

6. XP and the XP shop

  • You earn XP by studying. XP is a game score: it has no money value, cannot be bought, and cannot be sold, transferred or exchanged for money.
  • You can spend XP on items in the XP shop (for example avatars). Items have no money value either.
  • We may change XP rules, prices and items, set daily limits, and remove XP or items gained by cheating or by exploiting a bug.

7. What is not allowed

  • Cheating the XP or streak system, using bots, or creating fake accounts.
  • False or abusive mistake reports.
  • Trying to access other people's accounts or data, or attacking or overloading the service.
  • Using KampusLink to cheat during a real exam.
  • Anything illegal under Algerian law.

8. Suspension
