Delete Your Account

Last updated: 28 August 2026

You can delete your KampusLink account and data at any time, whether or not you still have the app installed.

Option 1: Delete in the app (instant)

Open KampusLink, go to Profile → Settings → Delete Account, and confirm. Your account is deleted immediately — no waiting period.

Option 2: Request deletion without the app

If you no longer have the app installed, email hello@kampus-link.com from the same address associated with your Google sign-in, with the subject line "Delete my account." We'll verify and process your request within 7 days.

What gets deleted

  • Your profile — username, university, speciality, avatar
  • Your community posts, comments, and likes
  • Your marketplace listings
  • Your messages, sent and received
  • Your blocks and reports you've filed
  • Your account credentials — you will no longer be able to sign in
Deletion is permanent and cannot be undone. Some limited records may be retained where needed for safety, legal, or fraud-prevention purposes, consistent with our Privacy Policy.

How long it takes

In-app deletion is immediate. Email requests are processed within 7 days of verification.

Questions about this process: hello@kampus-link.com
