Delete Your Account

Last updated: 15 November 2026

You can delete your KampusLink account and its data at any time, with or without the app installed.

Option 1: in the app (immediate)

Open KampusLink, go to Profile → Settings → Delete account and confirm.

Option 2: by email (without the app)

Send an email to kampuslink.team@kampus-link.com from the email address you sign in with, with the subject "Delete my account". A parent or legal guardian of a student under 18 can also ask; tell us the student's username. We confirm and delete within 7 days.

What is deleted

  • Your account and profile: name, email, username, avatar, study choices.
  • Your study progress: lessons, flashcards, quiz and exam results, study time, streak, XP and shop items.
  • Your reminder settings.
  • Your chat messages and the reports you sent.

Deletion is permanent and cannot be undone. Copies in our backups disappear when those backups expire. We may keep a minimal record only where the law requires it or to prevent fraud (see the Privacy Policy).
