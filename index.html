Everything you need to revise, in one app.

No more searching through PDFs and group chats the night before the exam. KampusLink follows the official programme of your year and breaks it into small steps you can finish on the bus.

Short lessons

Each lesson takes a few minutes: one idea, clear examples and worked solutions, one step at a time.

Flashcards that come back

Cards you get wrong come back soon; cards you know come back later. You spend your time on what you have not learned yet.

Exam practice

Quizzes after each lesson, exercises with corrections, and timed mock exams in the style of your real exams.

Made for how students in Algeria study.

Works offline

Download your modules once on Wi-Fi or mobile data, then study anywhere. Your progress syncs when you are back online.

Your language

Use the app in Arabic, French or English. Scientific terms are linked to the words you learned at lycée.

Streaks and XP

Keep a daily streak, earn XP while you study and spend it in the XP shop. Gentle reminders help you keep the habit; you can turn them off.
