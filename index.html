One app instead of five group chats and a bulletin board.

Most campus information in Algeria is scattered — a university announcement here, a Facebook group there, a WhatsApp forward that's three days old. KampusLink pulls it into a single, organized feed for your university, and does the translating so language isn't a barrier to knowing what's going on.

Campus news, translated

We follow official university sources and campus pages, and turn each update into a short, clear post — translated automatically so it reads naturally whether you follow it in French, Arabic, or English.

A community that's actually yours

Discussion tied to your university, not a random national group. Ask questions, share notes, find people in your program.

A marketplace for students

Buy and sell textbooks, gear, and housing leads with people from your own campus, instead of scrolling a citywide marketplace app.

We label where every post comes from.

Every campus-news item carries a trust label based on its source — an official university account is marked differently from an unverified page — so you can tell at a glance how much weight to give it. Nothing is published as news without a source attached.

Signing in with Google

KampusLink uses Google Sign-In so you can create an account without setting up another password. It also lets your feed, saved posts, and marketplace listings follow you if you switch phones.

We request your name, email address, and profile photo from Google to set up and identify your KampusLink account. We don't post on your behalf, and we don't access your Google Drive, Contacts, or Gmail.
