Privacy Policy

Last updated: 15 November 2026

The short version: KampusLink is a study app. We collect what we need to run your account and save your study progress. We do not sell your data, we do not show ads, and you can delete your account at any time.

1. Who we are

KampusLink is a study app for students in Algeria: university students and secondary-school (lycée) students preparing for the baccalauréat. It is run by OPERATOR: Mpofu Professor, Annaba Sidi Amar 19 mai, Algeria ("we", "us"). Contact: kampuslink.team@kampus-link.com.

KampusLink is independent. It is not affiliated with the Ministry of Higher Education, the Ministry of National Education, the ONEC, any university or any school.

2. What we collect

When you sign in with Google: your name, email address and Google account ID. Google gives us these; we never see your Google password.

What you tell us during setup: a username, your language, and what you study. University students: speciality, year and semester. Bac students: stream (filière) and year only. We do not ask for the name of your school.

Your study activity: lessons opened and finished, flashcard reviews, quiz and exercise answers, mock exam scores, study time, streak, XP, items you unlock in the XP shop, and your reminder settings. This is what lets the app work offline and keep your progress on a new phone.

What you send us: messages you post in module chats, mistake reports on content, and feedback you send from the app.

Technical data: crash reports and basic app diagnostics (device model, operating system, app version, time zone). The app version you install may also include Google Firebase Analytics, which can record basic usage events automatically. We do not use it for advertising, and we are removing it from the app.

We do not collect: your contacts, your photos, your precise location, your phone number, your date of birth or your school's name. We have no ads and no ad tracking.

3. Why we use it

  • To create your account and keep you signed in.
  • To show you the right modules and save and sync your progress, streak and XP.
  • To send study reminders you can turn off. Reminders are scheduled on your phone.
  • To run module chats, translate chat messages into your language, and moderate reports.
  • To fix bugs, keep the service secure and prevent abuse (for example fake XP or fake reports).
  • To answer you when you contact us.

We process your data because you agreed to it when you created your account, because we need it to provide the service you asked for, or because we must meet a legal obligation. You can withdraw your agreement at any time by deleting your account.

4. Who can see your data

  • Other students see only your username, your avatar from the XP shop and the chat messages you post. They never see your email address or your real name.
  • Service providers who run parts of the app for us, only to provide their service:
    • Supabase: database, sign-in and account storage.
    • Cloudflare: website hosting and file storage.
    • Google: Google sign-in, Google Play, Firebase Crashlytics (crash reports) and Firebase Analytics (while it is still in the app).
    • An AI translation provider, which receives chat messages to translate them.
    • Telegram and our email provider, which deliver the feedback you send us from the app to our team.
  • Authorities, only when Algerian law requires it.

We do not sell or rent your data to anyone.

5. Where your data is stored

Our providers store data on servers outside Algeria. Data is encrypted while it travels between your phone and our servers, and access is restricted so that each account can only read its own private data. We handle these transfers under the conditions of Law No. 18-07 on the protection of personal data, as amended.

6. Students under 18

Many bac students are under 18. If you are under 18, you may use KampusLink only with the permission of your parent or legal guardian. Your parent or guardian can contact us at any time to ask what data we hold about you or to have your account deleted.

For bac students we keep data to the minimum: no school name, no date of birth, no free profile photo and no private messages between students.

KampusLink is not meant for children under 13. If we learn that a child under 13 has an account, we delete it.

7. How long we keep data

  • Account and study progress: while your account exists.
  • Chat messages: while your account exists, unless they are removed for breaking the rules.
  • Feedback and mistake reports: only as long as we need them to fix the problem.
  • Crash reports: as long as our crash-reporting provider keeps them, which is a limited period.

When you delete your account, we delete your account and the data linked to it. Copies in our backups disappear when those backups expire. We may keep a minimal record only if the law requires it or to prevent fraud.

8. Your rights

You can, at any time:

  • ask what data we hold about you (access);
  • correct it (in the app, or by writing to us);
  • object to a use of your data, or withdraw your agreement;
  • delete your account in the app, or by email (see Delete Account);
  • turn off reminders in the app.

Write to kampuslink.team@kampus-link.com from the email address you sign in with. We answer within 30 days. You can also complain to Algeria's national authority for the protection of personal data (ANPDP).

9. Data breaches

If a security incident puts your data at risk, we will tell the national authority and the affected users, in plain words, as quickly as possible and within the time the law sets.

10. Changes

If we change this policy in an important way, we will tell you in the app before the change applies.
