Privacy Policy

Last updated: [DATE]

KampusLink ("we," "us," "the app") is a free platform helping African and international students navigate university and daily life in Algeria. This policy explains what we collect, why, and how you're in control of it.

1. What we collect

When you sign up:

  • Name, email address, and profile photo, provided by Google when you sign in with Google — this is the only way to create an account, so we never see or store a password.
  • Username, university, and speciality/major, which you provide during setup.
  • City, automatically set based on the university you select — we don't ask for this separately.

As you use the app:

  • Content you post — community posts, comments, marketplace listings, reports, and any photos you attach.
  • Messages you send to other students through the app.
  • Basic usage activity — what you view, save, tap, and confirm — used to personalize your feed. This never leaves an anonymized, aggregate-style profile; we don't sell or share individual behavior data.
  • Crash reports and app performance data, so we can fix issues.
We do not access your contacts, photo library beyond what you explicitly upload, or precise GPS location. Marketplace distance is calculated from your university/city, not live location tracking. We do not buy or sell personal data to advertisers or third parties.

2. Why we collect it

  • To personalize your feed to your university, speciality, and interests
  • To let you communicate with other students (messages, community, marketplace)
  • To verify and moderate content so the platform stays trustworthy
  • To fix bugs and improve the app
  • To meet legal and Play Store requirements

3. How your data is stored and protected

Your account data lives in a secured database, access-controlled so you can only see and edit what belongs to you. Photos and uploads are stored separately. Data in transit is encrypted. Time-sensitive content (alerts, listings) expires automatically so old data doesn't sit around indefinitely.

4. Who your data is shared with

  • Other students, for content you choose to make visible — your username, posts, listings, and message content with people you message.
  • Service providers that help run the app: our database/auth provider, our hosting/storage provider, Google (sign-in), Firebase (notifications, analytics, crash reporting), and our AI translation providers. They process data only to provide their service to us.
  • Nobody else. We do not sell data to advertisers or data brokers. Any future advertising will use broad, non-personal targeting only.

5. Your controls

  • Edit your profile anytime in Settings
  • Delete your account directly in the app (Settings → Delete Account)
  • Block and report any user, anytime
  • Turn off notifications anytime

6. Data retention

Time-sensitive content is automatically removed once it's no longer relevant. Account data is kept while your account is active, and deleted or anonymized when you delete your account, except where limited records are needed for safety, legal, or fraud-prevention purposes.

7. Children's privacy

KampusLink is intended for university students, generally 18 and older. We do not knowingly collect data from children under 13. If you believe a child has created an account, contact us and we'll remove it.

8. Changes to this policy

If this policy changes meaningfully, we'll notify you in the app before the change takes effect.
