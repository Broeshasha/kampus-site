Community Guidelines

Last updated: 15 November 2026

KampusLink chats are for helping each other study. Many students here are under 18, so the rules are strict.

The one-line version: be kind, stay on topic, and keep personal information private.

1. Stay on topic

Module chats are for questions and help about the module: lessons, exercises, exams, how to study. No ads, no spam, no chain messages.

2. Respect everyone

  • No insults, harassment, threats or mocking another student.
  • No hate or discrimination about origin, region, religion, gender, disability or anything else.
  • Disagree with ideas, not with people.

3. Keep personal information private

  • Do not share phone numbers, addresses, social-media accounts, photos of people or the name of your school, yours or anyone else's.
  • Do not ask other students for personal information or try to move a conversation to another app.
  • Never send money or codes to anyone you meet in the app.

4. Zero tolerance

Any sexual content, any content involving the exploitation of minors, violence, drugs or weapons leads to immediate removal of the account. Where the law requires it, we report it to the authorities.

5. Honest study

  • Do not post leaked exam papers or answers during a real exam.
  • Do not present a guess as an official fact (dates, results, rules). Say where your information comes from.
  • Do not copy and republish KampusLink content outside the app.

6. Reports

Use the report button on a message or on a piece of content. Report honestly: false reports are themselves a violation. You do not need to argue in the chat; just report and move on.

7. What happens when rules are broken

Depending on how serious it is: the message is removed, a warning, a chat restriction, or the account is deleted. Serious violations lead to immediate deletion.

8. Something is wrong?

If something in the app worries you, report it, block the person if you can, and tell a parent or a teacher you trust. You can also write to us at kampuslink.team@kampus-link.com.
