Community Guidelines

Last updated: 18 August 2026

KampusLink exists because random WhatsApp groups and scattered announcements weren't good enough. These guidelines keep it that way — useful, trustworthy, and safe. They apply to Home, Community, Marketplace, and Messages.

The one-line version: Be real, be helpful, be kind — and never pretend something is confirmed when it isn't.

1. Trust labels exist for a reason — respect them

Every piece of content is labeled Official, Verified, or Community Report. If you're sharing something you heard, saw, or weren't part of confirming yourself, it's a Community Report — don't present it as more certain than that. Deliberately mislabeling or spreading false information as fact, especially about safety, deadlines, or official processes, is taken seriously and can lead to removal.

2. Report accurately

When you submit a report, include what you actually know: what happened, where, and roughly when. Reports that turn out to be repeatedly false or exaggerated affect your ability to submit future reports.

3. Community space is for students, by students

  • Keep discussions respectful, even in disagreement.
  • Nationality threads exist to build genuine community — welcoming, not exclusionary.
  • No hate speech, harassment, discrimination, or targeting of individuals or groups, ever.
  • No sexual content, and zero tolerance for anything involving or directed at minors.
  • Helping other students is exactly what this space is for — consistently helpful, accurate contributors may be recognized for it.

4. Marketplace — deal in good faith

  • Describe items honestly, with real photos of the actual item.
  • No prohibited items — see Terms of Service for the full list.
  • Meet in safe, public, or well-populated locations. KampusLink does not mediate transactions or guarantee any listing.
  • Scamming, fake listings, or repeated no-shows will result in account restriction.

5. Messaging — respectfully, and only 1:1

  • Messages are for genuine conversation — coordinating a pickup, replying to a question, connecting with another student.
  • Don't use messaging for unsolicited spam or mass outreach.
  • If a conversation makes you uncomfortable, block and report — don't feel obligated to keep responding.
  • Be cautious with anyone you don't know yet, especially around money or personal details.

6. What gets you removed

  • Posting false information as fact, especially safety- or deadline-critical content
  • Harassment, hate speech, or targeting another person or group
  • Sexual or exploitative content, or anything involving minors
  • Scams, fraud, or repeated prohibited-item listings
  • Spam or automated/fake account behavior
  • Impersonating another person, university, or organization

We'd rather warn first where possible, but severe violations can result in immediate removal.

7. How to report something

Every user, post, listing, and message thread has a report option — use it. High-risk reports are reviewed quickly; you don't need to argue your case publicly, just flag it and move on.

8. This is a living document

As KampusLink grows across more universities and cities, these guidelines will grow with it. Material changes will be shared in-app.
